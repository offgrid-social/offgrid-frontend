# OFFGRID Governance

OFFGRID is governed openly, transparently, and in public.

This document defines how decisions are made, how power is distributed,
and how recognition works.

---

## Governance Philosophy

- Governance happens where users are: **in the feed**
- Everyone can participate
- No hidden decision-making
- No pay-to-win influence

OFFGRID prioritizes legitimacy over speed.

---

## Governance Posts

Governance decisions are published as special posts in the main feed.

These posts are clearly marked as:
> Governance Vote

They may include:
- Feature proposals
- UI/UX changes
- Roadmap priorities
- Policy changes

---

## Voting Rights

All users may vote, including anonymous users.

### Vote Weighting

| Role | Votes |
|----|------|
| Anonymous | 1 |
| Registered user | 1 |
| Supporter (financial) | +1 |
| Hardware supporter | +1 |
| Contributor | +1 |
| Top Contributor | +2 |

**Maximum votes per person are capped.**

Money, hardware, or status can never create unlimited influence.

---

## Transparency

- Final results are public
- Vote weighting is disclosed
- No secret admin votes
- No algorithmic bias

---

## Roles

### Admin
- Platform moderation
- System maintenance
- Security decisions

Admins do **not** gain extra voting power.

---

### Contributor
- Contributes code or documentation
- Listed publicly in the contributor list
- Does not imply moderation rights

---

### Top Contributor
- Awarded manually for exceptional, sustained contributions
- Requires admin and contributor consensus
- Badge can be revoked

---

### Hardware Supporter
- Provides compute, storage, or bandwidth
- Recognized publicly
- No special control rights

---

### Supporter (Financial)
- Supports OFFGRID via Bitcoin
- Listed publicly (optional anonymity)
- No monetary leaderboard

---

## Public Recognition

OFFGRID maintains three public lists:

1. Supporters (financial)
2. Contributors
3. Hardware Supporters

These lists exist for transparency and appreciation — not competition.

---

## Verification

Public figures and well-known creators may apply for verification.

Verification confirms identity, not authority.

- Manual review only
- Proof via external presence
- No automated verification

---

## Abuse Prevention

- Rate limiting on anonymous actions
- Manual moderation only
- No automated behavioral scoring

---

## Changes to Governance

This document itself can only be changed through a governance vote.

---

OFFGRID governance exists to protect the community —
not to optimize growth.
