# OFFGRID

**OFFGRID** is an open-source social platform combining short video, text, and images —  
without tracking, without algorithms, and without surveillance.

No data extraction.  
No engagement manipulation.  
No growth hacks.

Just people.

---

## What is OFFGRID?

OFFGRID is a privacy-first social platform inspired by TikTok, X, and Instagram —  
but designed around **user autonomy**, **open governance**, and **community-owned infrastructure**.

- No tracking
- No profiling
- No algorithmic feeds
- No ads

OFFGRID is built as a public digital good.

---

## Core Principles

### Privacy by Design
- No analytics
- No IP logging
- No behavioral tracking
- No shadow profiles

### Open Source
- Fully open-source (server, client, tooling)
- Auditable by anyone
- Licensed under AGPLv3

### Transparent Governance
- Platform decisions happen publicly
- Feature votes are shown directly in the feed
- Everyone can participate — even anonymous users

### No Algorithmic Manipulation
- Feeds are chronological
- No engagement-based ranking
- No “For You” optimization

---

## Features (Planned MVP)

- Short-form vertical video feed
- Text posts
- Image posts
- Likes & comments
- Account-based posting
- Anonymous read & like access
- In-feed governance polls

---

## Accounts

OFFGRID supports multiple participation levels:

- Anonymous users (read & like)
- Registered users (post & comment)
- Contributors (code & development)
- Hardware supporters (infrastructure)
- Financial supporters (Bitcoin)

Support never equals control.
